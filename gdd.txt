# lethimcook

# Type - Web/Multiplayer

# Genre - Survival (like "Don't Starve")

# Core game loop
Players will compete with each other to collect resources in a certain amount of time.

# World Overview
Players live in the world of Originals who demand sacrifice from the players.
Their main objective in this world is to feed Originals and keep them satiated.
If the players fail to feed Original they have to face Originals' "wrath" and die.


# Characters
1. Original - God like creature that demand sacrifice. If not fed unleashes wrath upon players.
There are different type of Original that demand different type of sacrifice every round.
2. Wrath - Enemies that can kill players, unleashed by originals. Like a meteor shower, zombies, slimes etc.
Players have the option to escape it. After some time the intensity increases until the player can no longer keep up.
3. Players - Can collect sacrifice, Stagger other players, Dash, Teleport etc.
4. Sacrifices - Collectible items, can move also like Chickens.
5. Coins - Coins are collectibles that can compensate for absent sacrifices for the player.

# Detailed Game Loop
* Map will have food items scattered all over the place.
* Players will have to collect it and craft recipes at certain places in the map.
* Map will have giant utensils. Player will drop their items to craft dishes.
* Once they get the dish they will offer it to Original. A map will have just one Original.
* Originals will have hunger meter which fills up once player drops dishes.
* If hunger meter is not filled in certain amount of time Original unleashes wrath.
* Last one surviving the game wins.
